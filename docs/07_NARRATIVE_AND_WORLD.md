# Gilded Depths — Narrative & World Bible

**Version:** 1.0 (Phase 1)  ·  **Owner:** Narrative Direction & Game Writing teams

> Tone target: *awe, mystery, and the warmth of trusted partnership under pressure.* Indiana-Jones wonder without the cruelty of horror.

## 1. Premise

The **Cartographers' Circle** is a guild that maps the un-mappable: the sealed ruins of a vanished civilization, the **Gildfolk**, who buried their greatest works behind puzzles only *a coordinated party* could ever pass — because they believed no truth should be trusted to one mind alone. You are a newly-paired expedition team, descending where solo treasure-hunters have only ever vanished.

## 2. Tone & voice

- **Wonder first.** Each ruin should make players gasp before it makes them think.
- **Partnership as theme.** The Gildfolk's central belief — *"the truth is shared or it is lost"* — is literally the game's mechanic and its heart.
- **Restrained, environmental storytelling.** No long cutscenes; lore lives in murals, tablets, and the architecture itself.

## 3. The world

- **The Gildfolk:** an ancient people who encoded knowledge so that retrieving it *required cooperation* — a society-wide safeguard against tyranny and greed. Their ruins are tests of trust.
- **The six ruins (= biomes):** Sunken Atrium (water/light), Mirror Catacomb (reflection/illusion), Root Cathedral (overgrown/organic), Ashfall Forge (heat/metal), Tidal Archive (timing/tides), and the **Gilded Vault** (capstone, all tools).
- **The Circle:** your guild HQ hub — a candle-lit map room where you choose expeditions, review the journal, and read decoded lore.

## 4. Characters

- **Players:** customizable Cartographers; personality is expressed through play and cosmetics, not scripted dialogue (keeps the focus on *your* conversation).
- **Archivist Wren (NPC):** your guild contact who briefs expeditions and reacts to discoveries via the journal — warm, curious, a little anxious for your safety.
- **The Gildfolk (implied):** never seen; known only through their murals, voices echoing in solved mechanisms, and the slow reveal of *why* they sealed everything away.

## 5. Narrative delivery & cadence

- **Pre-expedition:** 1–2 lines from Wren + an illustrated ruin card.
- **In-ruin:** environmental lore (murals the Brush reveals, tablets the Ledger decodes) — optional, never blocking.
- **Capstone arc:** across the six ruins, decoded tablets slowly reveal the Gildfolk's fate and the meaning of the Gilded Vault — a quiet, satisfying payoff rewarding players who read together.

## 6. Writing rules

1. Lore tablets are short (≤ 60 words) and reward being read aloud to teammates.
2. Never gate *mechanical* progress behind reading lore; lore is the reward, not the key.
3. Keep mystery: imply, don't explain. The best reveal is the players' own theory-crafting on voice chat.
4. Localize-friendly; symbol puzzles must not depend on a specific spoken language.

## 7. Localization scope (launch)

EN full; text loc for FR, DE, ES, PT-BR, zh-Hans, JA, KO post-launch by wishlist geography. Glyph systems are language-agnostic by design.
